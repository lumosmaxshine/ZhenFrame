ABOUT ME / 个人简介

对我来说，设计能把复杂想法变得更容易理解，化解限制，再找出满足创造欲的路径。我相信灵感藏在最平凡的地方，把这些想法变成可触摸的东西，是我一直好奇的事。

For me, design makes complex ideas easier to understand, overcomes limits, and opens room for creativity. Inspiration lives in ordinary things — turning them into something tangible is what keeps me curious.
